**Key Characteristics:**

1. **Event Horizon**: The point of no return around a black hole.
2. **Singularity**: The center of a black hole, where gravity is infinite.

**Types of Black Holes:**

1. **Stellar Black Holes**: Formed from star collapse.
2. **Supermassive Black Holes**: Found at galaxy centers, with millions or billions of times the mass of the sun.
3. **Intermediate-Mass Black Holes**: Between stellar and supermassive black holes.

**Observational Evidence:**

1. **X-rays and Gamma Rays**: Emitted by hot gas swirling around black holes.
2. **Star Motions**: Stars near a black hole move faster than those farther away.
3. **Gravitational Waves**: Ripples in space-time produced by black hole mergers.

**Theoretical Implications:**

1. **Time Dilation**: Time passes slower near a black hole due to strong gravity.
2. **Information Paradox**: The question of what happens to information contained in matter that falls into a black hole.
